Injury Tracking Application (ITA) 300A 
Summary Data Dictionary 
 
In May 2016, OSHA amended 29 CFR 1904.41 by publishing the first Improve Tracking of Workplace 
Injuries and Illnesses final rule. The first year of data collection (CY 2016 data) was conducted in August 
December 2017. Data are from OSHA Form 300A: Summary of Work-Related Injuries and Illnesses. 
Establishments with 250+ employees not listed in Exempt Industries list and establishments with 20-249 
employees listed in the 300A Table must electronically submit their 300A data to OSHA each year, and the 
data submitted by establishments to the ITA is available at [website].   
 
 
Data Element Definition Format 
ID A unique number for each 300A record. Numeric 
establishment_name The name of the establishment reporting data. 
[Must be unique to the establishment]  
Character 
establishment_ID A unique number for each establishment. This 
variable links the 300A summary data and 
300/301 case detail data. 
Numeric 
ein Employer Identification Number (EIN) is also 
known as Federal Tax Identification Number. 
Character 
company_name The name of the company that owns the 
establishment. 
Character 
street_address The street address of the establishment. Character 
city The city where the establishment is located. Character 
state The state or territory where the establishment is 
located. 
Character 
zip_code The full zip code for the establishment. Character 
naics_code The North American Industry Classification 
System (NAICS) co de  for the establishment. Data 
through 2022 uses a 2012 NAICS code. Data 
collected in 2023 and later uses a 2012, 2017, or 
2022 NAICS code.  
Numeric 
naics_year The calendar year reflecting the version of 
NAICS codes used by the establishment [2012, 
2017, or 2022] When a NAICS code has not 
changed, the most recent NAICS year is listed. 
Data submissions with invalid NAICS codes are 
shown as missing. 
Numeric 
industry_description The industry description for the establishment. Character 
establishment_type 1 = Not a government entity 
2 = State Government entity 
3 = Local Government entity 
Numeric

maximum number of employees who worked 
there at any point in the year. 
• 1 = < 20 employees

establishment. 
Numeric 
total_hours_worked The total hours worked by all employees. 
 
Numeric 
no_injuries_illnesses Whether the establishment had any OSHA 
recordable work-related injuries or illnesses 
during the year. 
• 1 = the establishment had injuries or 
illnesses 
• 2 = the establishment did not have injuries 
or illnesses
