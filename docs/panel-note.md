## Rules (fixed before any model was run)

- **Sectors:** industrial NAICS 2-digit codes 11, 21, 22, 23, 31, 32, 33, 42, 48, 49 (agriculture, mining, utilities, construction, manufacturing, wholesale, transport and warehousing).
- **One record per site per year:** where an establishment_ID appears twice in a year, the most recent submission is kept.
- **Linking:** same establishment_ID in consecutive years; otherwise a backup key of normalised name, 5-digit zip and 6-digit NAICS, used only where the key is unique in both years and neither record is already linked.
- **Cohort:** 20 or more employees, at least 20,000 hours worked, 100 to 8,760 hours per average employee, and a computable DART rate.
- **Usable outcome:** the linked next-year record meets the same hours rules.
- **Target:** next year's DART rate is above the 80th percentile of the site's 3-digit NAICS group in the current year (pooled to the 2-digit sector when the group has fewer than 200 sites). The threshold uses only current-year data.
