Executive finding

Top-band sites crossed the line about 16 times as often as bottom-band sites.

In 2023, 46.5% of band 5 sites saw next year's serious-injury (DART) rate cross the line, against 2.9% in band 1: about 16x (46.5 ÷ 2.9). The 2024 test repeated the pattern, 47.5% against 3.0%.

The line
The serious-injury (DART) rate that marked the worst 20% of a site's industry this year. A site crosses it when its rate next year is higher.
