What to do with it
Risk engineering
Survey band 5 first

In band 5, 46.5% of sites crossed the line in 2023 and 47.5% in 2024. In band 1 it was 2.9% and 3.0%. Start site visits at the top of the list.

Observed · both test years
Pricing
Price within the class, not only by it

Rating class alone ranks sites little better than a coin toss (AUC 0.59). Spreading premium by score cut premium in the wrong band from 27.5% to 10.0%.

Underwriting
Trust the ranking most on large sites

Scorecard AUC is 0.915 for sites with 250+ employees and 0.762 for sites with 20 to 249. Pair small-site scores with a survey.

Read with care
Associations, not causes

The score ranks sites. It does not show why injuries happen.

Reported injuries, not claims

Employer-reported OSHA counts. A DART rate is not a claim cost.

Pricing is simulated

Not real premiums, claims or loss ratios. Claim cost $50,076.

Two test years

Starting years 2023 and 2024. A solid check, not a long record.
